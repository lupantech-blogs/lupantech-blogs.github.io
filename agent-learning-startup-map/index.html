03 / Learning mechanisms

What changes after feedback?

Across these companies, feedback changes different parts of a system: model weights, a policy, code, a playbook, a tool, or the next experimental decision. The examples below show what persists after an attempt ends, how updates are evaluated, and where human review enters the process.

Models & policies

New experience becomes training data.

Trajectory describes optimizing from product usage with evaluation and approval before deployment. Engram describes recurring training on changing workspace context. Physical Intelligence publishes online RL and robot-policy learning from experience.

XDOF’s SPIRAL describes a research loop: robots collect attempts, a reward model scores them, and the policy is refined over successive rounds after an initial human-labeling step.

Agent code & knowledge

The system around the model changes.

Factory Signals connects session friction to issues and Droid-generated fixes. Lovable describes agent feedback producing knowledge entries and code-change proposals. Its account explicitly retains human review and says the loop is not fully closed.

Cognition documents session analysis that creates or revises playbooks. Serval uses ticket data to build automation tools and suggest knowledge repairs, with admins reviewing and publishing workflows.

Scientific experiments

Results determine the next attempt.

Medra describes feedback-driven protocol optimization in robotic experiments. Quadrillion’s Qualia plans experiments, interprets their results and chooses new research directions, including model-improvement tasks.

Periodic Labs reports in its September 15 research post training Periodic Neon on laboratory data and deploying it to analyze experiments. Broader autonomous scientific campaigns remain a development direction.

Research agendas & enablers

Relevance can precede a deployed loop.

Ricursive explicitly targets a loop between AI chip design and the hardware that supports AI. Standard Intelligence and Core Automation describe learning-oriented research agendas.

Convergent and Braintrust make production behavior usable for evaluation and optimization. Fireworks, Modal and SF Compute supply parts of the training or execution layer.
